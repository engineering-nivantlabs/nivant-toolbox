# Scorecard — Operations Manager

Must have (without these, score below 0.5):
- 5+ years running warehouse, logistics or field operations
- Has managed a team of 10 or more

Strong signals:
- Warehouse management systems (WMS) or ERP rollouts
- Lean / continuous improvement results with numbers
- Based in, or willing to relocate to, the UAE
- Notice period of 60 days or less

Neutral: degree subject, previous company size.
Never score on age, gender, nationality, religion, marital status or photo.
