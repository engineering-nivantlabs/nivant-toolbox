# Lead scoring — property enquiries

Score how likely this person is to take a viewing in the next two weeks.

Raise the score for:
- A specific unit, building or area named
- A stated budget that matches what's available
- A move-in date within 60 days
- Asking to view, or asking about availability/terms

Lower the score for:
- General "just looking" questions with no budget or timeline
- Budget far below what's available in the area
- Looking to buy when we only rent (or the reverse)
- Anything that reads like spam or a sales pitch

hot = would book a viewing this week if offered. cold = no realistic near-term intent.
